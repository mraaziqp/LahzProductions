## 3. What Each Admin Tab Is For

Overview
1. This is read-only.
2. It shows counts:
3. Projects = gallery jobs.
4. Services = services on website.
5. Reviews = client testimonials.

Portfolio
1. Add completed job photos to gallery.
2. Fill title and location.
3. Pick upload mode.
4. Upload photo(s).
5. Click Publish to Gallery.

Services
1. Add or edit services shown on website.
2. Fill title, category, icon, description.
3. Click Add Service.

Reviews
1. Add or edit client testimonials.
2. Fill client name, location, service rendered, quote.
3. Click Publish Review.

Job Tracker
1. Create jobs.
2. Update progress.
3. Upload work photos.
4. Send tracking link to customer on WhatsApp.

## 4. Job Tracker: Full Step-By-Step (For Moulana)

Step A: Create a new job
1. Open Job Tracker tab.
2. Click New Job.
3. Enter Client Name.
4. Enter Item Description.
5. Keep generated tracking code (or regenerate).
6. Click Create Job.

Step B: Send tracking link to customer
1. Find the job in the list.
2. Click green WhatsApp button.
3. Send the prepared message.

Step C: Update job while work is happening
1. Click the job card (or Update button) to open it.
2. Change status:
3. Scheduled = booked, not started.
4. Prepping/Stripping = preparation stage.
5. In Production = work in progress.
6. Quality Check = final checks.
7. Ready for Delivery = done.
8. Add note (optional, customer can see it).
9. Upload progress photo (optional, customer can see it).
10. Click Save Update.

Step D: Finish job
1. Set status to Ready for Delivery.
2. Message customer to collect/delivery.

Delete job (only if needed)
1. Click red bin icon.
2. Confirm delete.
3. Warning: delete cannot be undone.

## 5. What Customer Sees

Customer does not log in.

They either:
1. Click the WhatsApp tracking link you sent.
2. Or type tracking code in Track Your Order section.

They will see:
1. Their name and item.
2. Current status.
3. Progress bar.
4. Latest photo and note (if added).

## 6. Daily Routine (Very Short)

1. New order comes in.
2. Create job in Job Tracker.
3. Send WhatsApp tracking link.
4. Update status as work moves.
5. Upload photo/note when useful.
6. Mark Ready for Delivery when done.

## 7. Safety Notes

1. Changes are live immediately.
2. Deleting projects/services/reviews/jobs cannot be undone.
3. Keep job until customer has received item.
4. Log out when finished (Sign Out button).
