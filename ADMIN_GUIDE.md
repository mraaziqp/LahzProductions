# LAHZ Productions — Admin & Job Tracking Guide

---

## How to Log In

1. On the live website, scroll to the very bottom of the page and click the small **lock icon** (or "Admin" link).
2. Enter your admin **email address** and **password**.
3. Click **Sign In**. You will be taken straight into the Admin Dashboard.

> **Tip:** Only the account registered in Supabase can log in. If you forget your password, reset it from the Supabase dashboard at supabase.com.

---

## The Admin Dashboard — 5 Sections

Once you are logged in you will see a dark sidebar on the left with five sections. Here is what each one does:

---

### 1. Overview (Business Overview)

**What it is:**
A live summary of how much content is on your website right now.

**What you will see:**
| Card | What it counts |
|---|---|
| Projects Showcased | Total before/after jobs in your portfolio gallery |
| Active Services | Services listed on the Services section of the site |
| Client Reviews | Testimonials shown on the website |

**What to do here:** Nothing — this is read-only. Use it to quickly check if everything looks right.

---

### 2. Portfolio (Portfolio Management)

**What it is:**
This is where you add photos of completed jobs to the gallery that visitors see on the website.

**Step-by-step to add a new portfolio project:**

1. Click **Portfolio** in the left sidebar.
2. Fill in **Project Title** — e.g. "3-Seater Grey Couch Restoration".
3. Fill in **Location** — e.g. "Sea Point" or "Cape Town".
4. Choose an **image mode** (see below).
5. Drag & drop your photo(s) into the upload boxes, or click them to browse your phone/computer.
6. Choose a **Category** — either "Cleaning" or "Craftsmanship".
7. Click the big **Publish to Gallery** button.

**Image Mode options explained:**

| Mode | When to use it |
|---|---|
| **Before / After Slider** | You have TWO photos — one before the job, one after. Visitors drag a slider to compare them. **Best option for most jobs.** |
| **Featured Photo** | You already have a single photo where before & after are side-by-side in one image (you combined them yourself). |
| **Single Photo** | Just one photo — no comparison. Good for general portfolio shots. |
| **After Only** | Only the finished result photo. It shows with a teal "After" badge. |
| **Before Only** | Only the original condition photo. It shows with a grey "Before" badge. |

**To delete a project:**
Scroll down to the "Current Projects" list and click the **red bin icon** on the right of any project. You will be asked to confirm.

---

### 3. Services (Service Catalog)

**What it is:**
The list of services that appears on the "Services" section of the website. Customers read these to understand what LAHZ Productions offers.

**Step-by-step to add a new service:**

1. Click **Services** in the sidebar.
2. Fill in **Title** — e.g. "Leather Care & Repair".
3. Choose a **Category**:
   - **Restoration** — repairing or restoring existing items
   - **Manufacturing** — building or making new items
   - **SpecializedCare** — specialist treatments like deep cleaning
4. Choose an **Icon** — pick whichever icon best represents the service (e.g. "Sofa" for upholstery, "Wind" for cleaning).
5. Write a **Short Description** — one sentence explaining the service.
6. Click **Add Service**.

**To edit a service:**
Click the **Edit** button on any service in the list, make your changes, then click **Save**.

**To delete a service:**
Click the **red bin icon** on the right of any service. You will be asked to confirm.

---

### 4. Reviews (Client Testimonials)

**What it is:**
The customer review quotes that cycle through on the website homepage.

**Step-by-step to add a new review:**

1. Click **Reviews** in the sidebar.
2. Fill in **Client Name** — e.g. "Fatima Moosa".
3. Fill in **Location** — e.g. "Cape Town" or "Johannesburg".
4. Fill in **Service Rendered** — what job was done, e.g. "3-Seater Couch Restoration".
5. Write the **Quote** — exactly what the client said.
6. Click **Add Review**.

**To edit or delete a review:** Use the **Edit** or **bin** buttons next to each review in the list.

---

### 5. Job Tracker (Job Manager)

**What it is:**
The most important tool for day-to-day use. This lets you:
- Create a new job for every custom order you receive.
- Update the status of a job as it moves through your workshop.
- Upload a progress photo so the client can see the work.
- Send the client a WhatsApp message with a personal tracking link.

> **The client does NOT need to log in or create an account.** They just click the link and see the live status of their order.

---

## How the Job Tracking System Works — Step by Step

### Moulana's Side (Admin)

**Step 1 — Create the job**
1. Click **Job Tracker** in the left sidebar.
2. Click the green **New Job** button.
3. A popup will appear. Fill in:
   - **Client Name** — e.g. "Ahmed Salie"
   - **Item Description** — e.g. "2-Seater Brown Couch Recushioning"
4. A **6-character tracking code** is automatically generated (e.g. `XK7M2P`). You can click "Regenerate" if you want a different code.
5. Click **Create Job**. The job now appears in the list.

**Step 2 — Send the tracking link to the client**
1. Find the job in the list.
2. Click the **green WhatsApp button** on the right side of the job card.
3. WhatsApp will open with a pre-written message like:
   > "Salaam/Hi Ahmed! Your custom order with LAHZ Productions is in our system. You can track its live progress here: https://lahzproductions.com/?track=XK7M2P"
4. Send it to the client.

> You can also click the **copy icon** to copy just the tracking code and share it another way.

**Step 3 — Update the job status as work progresses**
1. Click anywhere on a job card to expand it.
2. Change the **Status** dropdown to the current stage:
   - **Scheduled** — Job is booked, not started yet
   - **Prepping / Stripping** — Item received, being stripped and prepared
   - **In Production** — Your craftsmen are actively working on it
   - **Quality Check** — Almost done, going through final inspection
   - **Ready for Delivery** — Job is complete and ready for collection or delivery
3. (Optional) Type a **note** — e.g. "Foam replaced, working on the fabric now".
4. (Optional) Click **Upload Photo** to attach a work-in-progress photo.
5. Click **Save Update**. The client's tracking page updates instantly.

**Step 4 — When the job is done**
Once the status is set to **Ready for Delivery**, the client's tracking page will show the job as complete. You can send them another WhatsApp message to let them know.

**To delete a job:**
Click the **red bin icon** on a job card. This will permanently remove it. You will be asked to confirm first.

---

### The Client's Side (Customer)

The customer receives a WhatsApp link. When they click it, they land on the LAHZ Productions website and immediately see their job's tracking page. No login needed.

**What the customer sees:**

1. **Job header** — their name and the description of their item.
2. **Progress tracker** — a visual step-by-step bar showing all 5 stages:
   - Scheduled → Prepping → In Production → Quality Check → Ready for Delivery
   - The current stage is highlighted in teal.
   - Completed stages show a green tick.
3. **Latest Update** — if Moulana added a photo or note, it appears here. The customer can see the photo of their item and read the update message.

The customer can also visit the website themselves at any time and enter their tracking code manually in the **"Track Your Order"** section of the homepage.

## Quick Reference — Daily Workflow

```
New order received
        ↓
Click "New Job" → fill in name & item → Create Job
        ↓
Click WhatsApp button → send tracking link to client
        ↓
As work progresses → click job → update Status + add photo/note → Save Update
        ↓
Client sees live updates on their tracking page instantly
        ↓
Set status to "Ready for Delivery" → WhatsApp the client to collect
```

## Other Tools on the Website

### Quote Estimator
Visitors on the website can fill in a quote form (Deep Cleaning or Upholstery/Repair). When they submit it, a pre-filled WhatsApp message is sent directly to your number so you can respond with a price. **You do not need to manage this in the admin panel** — it works automatically.

### Before/After Gallery Slider
Visitors can drag a slider left and right to compare before and after photos of your work. These come directly from the projects you upload in the **Portfolio** section.

---

## Tips & Important Notes

- **All changes are live immediately** — when you publish a project, add a service, or update a job status, it appears on the website straight away. There is no "publish" or "approve" step.
- **Images are stored securely** — all photos are uploaded to Supabase storage (cloud). They will not be lost if your device is damaged.
- **Job tracking codes are permanent** — once you create a job and send the code to a client, do not delete the job until the order is fully complete and collected.
- **Only delete things you are sure about** — deletions cannot be undone.
- **To go back to the live website** — click the **"View Live Site"** button at the bottom of the left sidebar.
- **To log out** — click the red **Sign Out** button at the bottom of the left sidebar.
